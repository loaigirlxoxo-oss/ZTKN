## Theme

Acid hazard terminal frame.

This theme is a black technical monitor panel with toxic lime, cyan, and magenta splatter accents. It should feel like a warning display, maintenance terminal, or industrial diagnostic screen that has been stickered and stained, while still keeping the main sensor zone readable.

## Design Intent

The design should keep the strong rectangular technical border, warning-strip corners, barcode blocks, diagonal hazard bands, and dirty ink accents. The center stays dark and open so AIDA64 values can sit clearly on top.

Important visual cues:

- Black monitor panel with thin technical linework.
- Acid lime warning marks and diagonal hazard stripes.
- Cyan and magenta splatter used as accent damage, not as the main structure.
- Barcode blocks, small terminal labels, and industrial panel edges.
- High contrast edges with a dark clean sensor field.

## Asset Direction

Frames should look like pieces of the same warning terminal: broken technical rectangles, barcode plates, diagonal stripe plates, clipped corners, and small industrial labels. Circle frames should resemble warning scanner rings or diagnostic ports.

Circle gauges should use segmented hazard arcs and thermal color progression. Horizontal gauges should use warning rails, barcode ticks, and controlled color fills that remain readable.

## Do Not

- Do not turn it into a plain green terminal.
- Do not make splatter dominate the whole asset.
- Do not use soft watercolor ink.
- Do not remove the industrial warning-frame structure.
- Do not make line colors fight the gauge fill colors.
