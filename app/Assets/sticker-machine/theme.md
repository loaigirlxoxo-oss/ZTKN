## Theme

Black-and-white sticker machine collage.

This theme is a dense monochrome sticker wall with a dark usable sensor window. It should feel like print labels, barcode stickers, tape, torn decals, graffiti marks, QR-like blocks, and icon stickers arranged around the border.

## Design Intent

The main personality is the black-and-white collage density. Color should be sparse and intentional, used only as small accent stickers or warning marks. The center should stay dark, framed by chaotic sticker material.

Important visual cues:

- Black, white, and paper-like sticker contrast.
- Barcodes, labels, stamps, tape strips, QR-like blocks.
- Torn paper edges and sticker overlap.
- Small accent color only where needed.
- A dark central panel left clean for AIDA64 values.

## Asset Direction

Frames should look like sticker cutouts, taped labels, barcode plates, and torn paper components. Circle frames should resemble sticker rings or label seals. Rectangle and square frames should use layered decal borders, not clean vector boxes.

Circle gauges should be label-like meters with barcode ticks or stamped segments. Horizontal gauges should use sticker strips, barcode fills, or torn tape progress shapes.

## Do Not

- Do not make it colorful like candy-hazard.
- Do not turn it into polished tech glass.
- Do not remove the collage/sticker density from the border.
- Do not use smooth generic rounded cards.
- Do not put heavy sticker noise over the center reading area.
