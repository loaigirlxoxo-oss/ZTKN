## Theme

Navy and gold magic-circle inspired panel.

This theme is a dark navy magical instrument panel with antique gold ornament, constellation lines, compass geometry, and elegant occult chart details. It should feel ritualistic and premium, but still structured enough for a sensor panel.

## Design Intent

The design should preserve the navy-and-gold occult instrument feeling and the central framed sensor area. Gold linework should be precise, ornate, and layered. Rings are important assets, but they must be separated into frames or gauges. Backgrounds must not include fixed circular placement guides, large rings, sockets, or arcs that force sensor placement.

Important visual cues:

- Deep navy star-field background.
- Thin ornamental borders and corner geometry.
- Constellation-like straight lines, diamond star marks, compass ticks, and angular chart geometry.
- Symmetrical composition with broad dark placement freedom for sensors.
- No baked-in circular gauge positions in the background.

## Asset Direction

Frames should be extracted or rebuilt as movable gold ornament pieces from this visual language. Circle frames should preserve the magic-circle structure: nested rings, radial ticks, small symbols, star nodes, and compass points. Rectangle and square frames should use thin gold borders and decorative corner geometry.

Circle gauges should feel like magic-circle meters. Horizontal gauges should look like gold measuring rails, constellation progress lines, or enchanted instrument scales.

## Do Not

- Do not replace the ornate gold rings with generic circles.
- Do not flatten the design into simple yellow lines.
- Do not make it cyber neon.
- Do not bake ring elements into the background.
- Do not put large circles, arcs, round sockets, or circular placement guides in backgrounds.
- Do not add pop/splatter elements to this theme.
