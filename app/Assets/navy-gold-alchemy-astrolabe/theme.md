## Theme

Navy and gold alchemy astrolabe panel.

This theme is a denser astronomical instrument design: astrolabe rings, constellation networks, small planetary nodes, angular gold rails, and asymmetric technical ornamentation. It is related to `navy-gold-magic-circle`, but this one should feel more mechanical, scholarly, and instrument-like.

## Design Intent

The design should preserve the gold astrolabe complexity and the constellation network across the panel. The circular elements should be treated as movable frame/gauge material where possible. The center must remain usable for sensor values, with ornamentation reinforcing the display rather than covering it.

Important visual cues:

- Deep navy background with star texture.
- Gold astrolabe rings and measurement ticks.
- Constellation lines and node points.
- Mechanical/academic instrument geometry.
- More asymmetry and density than the magic-circle theme.

## Asset Direction

Frames should look like detachable astrolabe parts, measuring rails, star-map brackets, and gold instrument plates. Circle frames should use dense ticks, nested rings, pointer marks, and orbit lines. Rectangle and square frames should use alchemical diagram borders and measurement geometry.

Circle gauges should behave like astrolabe indicators or rotating instrument dials. Horizontal gauges should look like gold scale rulers, celestial measurement bars, or constellation-linked progress rails.

## Do Not

- Do not make it identical to `navy-gold-magic-circle`.
- Do not simplify it into plain gold rectangles.
- Do not add neon arcade colors.
- Do not bake every circular ornament into the background only.
- Do not remove the star-map and measurement-instrument feeling.
