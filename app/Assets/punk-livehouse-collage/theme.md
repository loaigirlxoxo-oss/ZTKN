# punk-livehouse-collage

## Theme

Late-70s punk livehouse flyer wall for an AIDA64 sensor panel.

The design should feel like a dirty venue wall covered with torn gig posters,
ripped tape, xerox halftone, staples, safety pins, checker fragments, caution
stripes, and ransom-note-like paper shapes. It should carry band-poster energy
without using real band names, logos, album art, readable slogans, or portraits.

## Intent

- Keep the sensor panel dark enough to read values.
- Put collage density on the edges and corners, leaving usable sensor space.
- Use black, off-white paper, hazard yellow, hot red, cyan, and dirty gray.
- Let high values move toward orange and red.
- Make gauges feel like pasted punk UI parts, not clean corporate controls.

## Asset Direction

- Backgrounds: wide 1920x480 livehouse collage, no side blur fill.
- Frames: transparent center holes, torn-poster edges, tape, staples, print grit.
- Circle gauges: open center, segmented punk meter, colored states.
- Horizontal gauges: opaque dark bed under the moving bar, no half-transparent slot.

## Avoid

- Real band names, logos, album covers, readable copyrighted poster text.
- Character mascots or real faces.
- Clean neon sci-fi UI.
- Empty JSON-like abstract line art.
- Preview, mockup, source, or contact-sheet files inside the theme folder.
