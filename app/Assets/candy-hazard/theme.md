## Theme

Pop candy hazard panel.

This theme is the most playful Splatoon-like direction: candy colors, rounded bubbles, paint drips, checker patterns, hazard stripes, cones, and sticker-like edge decoration. It should feel bright and chaotic around the border, but the center must remain usable as a sensor display area.

## Design Intent

The design should preserve the contrast between the clean dark center and the colorful border. The visual energy belongs mostly to the edges: paint splashes, candy blobs, rounded stickers, checkers, and safety signage.

Important visual cues:

- Pink, mint, lime, orange, and black checker accents.
- Rounded candy bubbles and sticker shapes.
- Paint drips and splashes at the border.
- Hazard stripes and traffic-cone style markers.
- A wide dark center reserved for sensor readability.

## Asset Direction

Frames should look like pop-sticker pieces, not polished UI cards. Circle frames can use bubble clusters, paint-ring edges, and checker fragments. Rectangle and square frames should combine soft candy silhouettes with hazard tape and sticker outlines.

Circle gauges should feel like pop dials or sticker meters. Horizontal gauges should look like candy rails, striped progress stickers, or ink-filled capsules.

## Do Not

- Do not make it clean corporate UI.
- Do not make the full center colorful.
- Do not replace the candy/hazard mix with generic neon.
- Do not make hard sci-fi panels the main language.
- Do not let decoration make sensor text unreadable.
